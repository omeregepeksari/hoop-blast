# 🏀 Hoop Blast

A basketball-themed take on the Block Blast puzzle game. Drag pieces made of basketballs onto an 8×8 hardwood court, fill full rows or columns to clear them, and keep the streak going.

**▶ Play it: https://omeregepeksari.github.io/hoop-blast/**

## How to play
- Drag any of the three pieces from the bench onto the court.
- Fill a full **row or column** to clear it.
- Clear on back-to-back moves to build a **streak** (heating up → on fire) for bonus points. Go 3 moves without clearing and the streak resets.
- Clear the whole court for a **Nothing But Net** +300 bonus.
- When none of your pieces fit, it's the final buzzer.

| Clear | Call |
|---|---|
| 1 line | SWISH! |
| 2 lines | AND ONE! |
| 3 lines | FROM DOWNTOWN! |
| 4 lines | SLAM DUNK! |
| 5+ lines | POSTERIZED! |

Works with mouse and touch. Best score is saved in your browser.

## Run locally
It's a single `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser.
